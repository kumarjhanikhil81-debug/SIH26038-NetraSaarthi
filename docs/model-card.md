## 2. Model Architecture

NetraSaarthi utilizes transfer learning built upon the **EfficientNet-B0** convolutional neural network architecture:

- **Backbone**: EfficientNet-B0 pre-trained on ImageNet-1K (`weights=EfficientNet_B0_Weights.DEFAULT`), leveraging compound coefficient scaling across depth, width, and resolution.
- **Feature Extractor**: Mobile Inverted Bottleneck Convolution (MBConv) blocks with Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE) optimization.
- **Final Convolutional Stage**: `features.8` (Conv2dNormActivation outputting 1280 feature channels).
- **Classification Head**:
  ```python
  nn.Sequential(
      nn.Dropout(p=0.4),
      nn.Linear(in_features=1280, out_features=5)
  )
  ```
- **Explainability Layer**: Custom forward and backward gradient hooks attached to `backbone.features.8` to compute Gradient-weighted Class Activation Maps (Grad-CAM).

---

## 3. Intended Use

- **Primary Application**: Assistive clinical decision support and preliminary triage of Diabetic Retinopathy (DR) in rural, underserved Indian primary healthcare setups (**Ayushman Arogya Mandirs** and outreach screening camps).
- **Intended Users**: Trained frontline health workers (ASHA, ANM, Community Health Officers) operating under the supervision of Medical Officers and tele-ophthalmologists.
- **Triage Role**: Flagging referable cases (ICDR Grades 2, 3, 4) to prioritize appointments at Community Health Centers (CHCs) and District Hospitals, while confirming non-referable cases (Grades 0, 1) for routine periodic follow-up.

---

## 4. Out-of-Scope Uses

- **Autonomous Clinical Diagnosis**: The model must **never** make unverified medical diagnoses without human ophthalmologist confirmation.
- **Pediatric Screening**: The model is not trained or calibrated for retinopathy of prematurity (ROP) or pediatric retinal conditions.
- **Non-Diabetic Retinopathy Diagnosis**: The model must not be used to detect or rule out other retinal pathologies, such as glaucoma, age-related macular degeneration (AMD), or retinal detachment.
- **Non-Retinal Imagery**: Any application to anterior segment, external eye, or non-ophthalmic photographs is completely unsupported.
- **Emergency Acute Vision Loss Triage**: Not intended for acute ocular emergencies (e.g., central retinal artery occlusion or chemical injuries).

---

## 5. Input

- **Modality**: Digital Color Retinal Fundus Photography (CFP).
- **Field of View (FOV)**: $45^\circ$ to $50^\circ$ centered on the macula and optic nerve head.
- **Input Dimensions**: Resized and cropped to $224 \times 224 \times 3$ pixels.
- **Data Type**: Float32 tensor scaled to $[0.0, 1.0]$.
- **Normalization**: Normalized using ImageNet channel statistics:
  - Mean ($\mu$): `[0.485, 0.456, 0.406]`
  - Standard Deviation ($\sigma$): `[0.229, 0.224, 0.225]`
- **Pre-Filtering**: Images must satisfy the heuristic quality gate ($\ge 180 \times 180$, adequate brightness, contrast, and sharpness) prior to tensor generation.

---

## 6. Output

The model outputs a structured JSON response containing:

1. **`predicted_class`** (`int`): Predicted ICDR severity stage (`0, 1, 2, 3, 4`).
2. **`confidence`** (`float`): Softmax probability associated with the top predicted class ($0.0 \le \text{confidence} \le 1.0$).
3. **`probabilities`** (`dict`): Full 5-class softmax probability distribution across all ICDR grades.
4. **`heatmap_url`** (`string`): Static URL path pointing to the synthesized Grad-CAM visual attention overlay.
5. **`recommendation`** (`string`): Clinical referral advice mapped to the predicted severity tier.
6. **`disclaimer`** (`string`): Mandatory clinical decision support disclaimer.

---

## 7. Diabetic Retinopathy Classes

The model classifies fundus imagery into the 5 stages defined by the **International Clinical Diabetic Retinopathy (ICDR)** scale:

| Class ID | Diagnosis | Primary Retinal Microvascular Pathologies | Referral Tier |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **0** | **No DR** | Absence of microaneurysms, hemorrhages, or exudates. | Non-Referable (Annual follow-up) |
| **1** | **Mild NPDR** | Microaneurysms (MAs) only. | Non-Referable (6–12 month follow-up) |
| **2** | **Moderate NPDR** | Microaneurysms, scattered blot hemorrhages, hard lipid exudates, cotton-wool spots. | Referable (4–8 weeks) |
| **3** | **Severe NPDR** | **4-2-1 Rule**: $>20$ intraretinal hemorrhages in 4 quadrants, venous beading in $\ge 2$ quadrants, or prominent IRMA in $\ge 1$ quadrant. | Referable (Urgent: 2–4 weeks) |
| **4** | **Proliferative DR (PDR)** | Neovascularization (NVD/NVE), preretinal/vitreous hemorrhage, fibrovascular proliferation. | Referable (Emergency: 24–48 hours) |

## 9. Preprocessing

Images pass through an ophthalmic vision standardization pipeline (`ml/preprocess.py`):

1. **Retinal Auto-Cropping**: Identifies circular retinal perimeter (intensity threshold $>7$) and crops away black non-informative borders.
2. **Ben Graham's Technique**:
   $$\text{enhanced} = 4 \cdot \text{image} - 4 \cdot \text{GaussianBlur}(\text{image}, \sigma=10) + 128$$
   Standardizes exposure across varied clinical cameras and amplifies microvascular lesion contrast.
3. **Resizing & Normalization**: Scaled to $224 \times 224 \times 3$ with standard ImageNet channel normalization.

---

## 10. Training Methodology

- **Framework**: PyTorch 2.x with Torchvision.
- **Transfer Learning Strategy**: Two-stage progressive fine-tuning:
  1. *Stage 1 (Warmup)*: Backbone frozen; classification head trained with learning rate $1 \times 10^{-4}$ for 2 epochs.
  2. *Stage 2 (Fine-Tuning)*: Full network unfrozen with differential learning rates:
     - Feature extractor backbone: $1 \times 10^{-5}$ (preserves low-level edge/texture features).
     - Classifier head: $1 \times 10^{-4}$ (adapts to 5-class clinical boundaries).
- **Imbalance Handling**:
  - `WeightedRandomSampler` with inverse class frequencies ($w_i = \frac{1}{N_{c(i)}}$).
  - Class-weighted Cross-Entropy loss ($W_c = \frac{N}{K \cdot N_c}$).
- **Optimizer**: Adam / AdamW with weight decay $1 \times 10^{-4}$.
- **Batch Size**: 16.
- **Loss Function**: Weighted Multi-Class Cross-Entropy Loss.

## 20. Explainability Using Grad-CAM

NetraSaarthi rejects "black-box" medical predictions by implementing **Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping (Grad-CAM)**:

```
[EfficientNet-B0 Backbone] ──▶ [features.8 Layer] ──▶ [Target Class Gradients] ──▶ [Grad-CAM Heatmap]
                                                                                          │
                                                                                          ▼
                                            [Interactive Opacity Slider in UI] ◀──────────┘
```

1. **Mechanism**:
   - Hooks capture activation feature maps from the final convolutional block (`features.8`).
   - Backward gradients for the predicted class are spatially pooled to calculate importance weights for each feature map.
   - Weighted activations are rectified with ReLU, normalized to $[0.0, 1.0]$, and rendered using a high-contrast colormap.
2. **Clinical Utility**:
   - Highlights focal microvascular lesions (microaneurysms, hemorrhages, exudates, and neovascular nets).
   - Allows ophthalmologists to verify whether the AI is attending to genuine pathology or optical camera artifacts.
3. **Clinical Disclaimer**:
   - The Grad-CAM heatmap represents model attention, **not** a pixel-precise anatomical lesion segmentation.

## 21. Known Limitations

1. **Prototype Maturity**: Not cleared as a medical software application by any regulatory authority.
2. **Peripheral Blind Spots**: Evaluates central $45^\circ$–$50^\circ$ fields only; cannot evaluate far-peripheral retinal neovascularization.
3. **Optical Media Opacities**: Cataracts, vitreous haze, or corneal scarring degrade image sharpness and may trigger quality gate rejections.
4. **Multi-Condition Blindness**: Model is insensitive to non-DR retinal conditions (e.g., glaucoma, AMD, epiretinal membrane).

---

## 22. Dataset Bias

- **Geographic & Demographic Concentration**: The primary benchmark dataset (APTOS 2019) was collected from eye hospitals in Tamil Nadu, India. Performance across populations with different iris pigmentation or systemic risk profiles may vary.
- **Hospital Selection Bias**: Training sets drawn from eye hospitals have higher disease severity prevalence than general rural community screening cohorts.

---

## 23. Image-Quality Limitations

Fundus image clarity heavily impacts feature extraction accuracy:
- **Motion Blur**: Defocused images obscure microaneurysms, leading to false negatives.
- **Underexposure**: Insufficient illumination renders the fundus too dark to differentiate hemorrhages from background pigment.
- **Glare & Flash Reflection**: Saturated pixels destroy macula details.
- **Heuristic Quality Gate Mitigation**: Evaluates Resolution, Luminance, Contrast, and Laplacian blur variance (`ml/quality.py`) to intercept degraded captures (`RETAKE_REQUIRED`) before inference.

---

## 24. Human Oversight

NetraSaarthi enforces strict **Human-in-the-Loop** operational constraints:
- **Triage Only**: Frontline health workers use predictions solely to establish referral timelines and provide basic counseling.
- **Specialist Clinical Override**: Ophthalmologists review the original fundus photograph alongside the Grad-CAM visualization, with full authority to modify the disease grade, add clinical notes, and sign off electronically.
- **Formal Sign-Off**: The final diagnosis recorded in the patient record is formally attributed to the reviewing clinician.

---

## 25. Privacy Considerations

- **De-Identification**: All personal data is decoupled from image records; images are indexed strictly by anonymous identifiers (`id_code` or `PAT-2026-XXX`).
- **Metadata Scrubbing**: EXIF metadata, camera serial numbers, and capture locations are stripped during upload.
- **Offline Vault Security**: Local browser storage caches only non-PII technical records; names, phone numbers, and addresses are never stored in client browser storage.
- **Compliance Alignment**: Conforms with India's **Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA 2023)** and **ABDM** data privacy principles.

---

## 26. Clinical Validation Requirements

Before NetraSaarthi can transition from a prototype to clinical deployment, the following formal validation steps are mandatory:

1. **Institutional Ethics Committee (IEC) Clearance**: Approval from relevant institutional review boards.
2. **Multi-Center Prospective Clinical Trial**: Validation across diverse geographic sites, camera hardware, and patient demographics in primary healthcare settings.
3. **Multi-Reader Ground Truth**: Comparison against consensus grading from at least 3 independent retinal specialists.
4. **Regulatory Approval**: Medical device clearance from the **Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation (CDSCO)** under Indian Medical Device Rules.
