# NetraSaarthi (नेत्रसारथी) — Project Overview
### Explainable AI for Diabetic Retinopathy Screening in Rural India
**Smart India Hackathon (SIH) Technical & Design Document**

---

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Prototype & Clinical Disclaimer**  
> NetraSaarthi is an **AI-assisted screening and clinical decision-support prototype** developed for research, demonstration, and triage workflow innovation. It is **not a clinically validated or certified diagnostic medical device**. The artificial intelligence predictions and visual attention maps generated by NetraSaarthi are intended solely to assist trained healthcare workers and medical officers in prioritizing patient referrals; they do **not** replace the clinical judgment, formal evaluation, or definitive diagnosis of a certified ophthalmologist.

---

## 1. Project Overview

**NetraSaarthi (नेत्रसारथी)** is an offline-first, explainable artificial intelligence screening and clinical decision-support platform engineered to address the growing challenge of Diabetic Retinopathy (DR) in rural and underserved communities across India. 

Designed for deployment at grassroots **Ayushman Arogya Mandirs** (Primary Health Centers and Sub-Centers) and mobile outreach camps, NetraSaarthi empowers frontline healthcare workers—such as Accredited Social Health Activists (**ASHA**) and Auxiliary Nurse Midwives (**ANM**)—to capture retinal fundus images, conduct instant heuristic quality checks, generate explainable AI risk assessments, and streamline tele-ophthalmology referrals to District Hospitals.

By combining deep learning feature extraction with **Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping (Grad-CAM)**, heuristic pre-screening image quality gating, bilingual voice guidance, and an offline-first Progressive Web Application (PWA) architecture, NetraSaarthi aims to make diabetic eye screening accessible, interpretable, and resilient in low-resource environments.

---

## 2. Problem Statement

Diabetic Retinopathy is one of the leading causes of preventable adult visual impairment and blindness globally. In India, where tens of millions of individuals live with diabetes, the public health burden is compounded by severe structural challenges:

1. **Acute Specialist Shortage in Rural Areas**: Over 70% of India's eye care specialists practice in major urban centers. In rural and remote regions, the ratio of ophthalmologists to population is critically low, often forcing rural patients to travel 50 to 150 kilometers simply to access basic dilated eye examinations.
2. **Asymptomatic Early Progression**: Diabetic Retinopathy remains largely asymptomatic in its early stages (Mild and Moderate NPDR). Without proactive, localized screening, patients frequently seek medical attention only when irreversible vision loss, maculopathy, or vitreous hemorrhage has already developed.
3. **Training Barriers at the Primary Care Level**: Frontline workers (ASHA/ANM) at health sub-centers possess strong community trust but lack specialized ophthalmic training to read complex retinal photographs or operate sophisticated slit-lamp equipment.
4. **"Black-Box" AI Distrust**: Many existing computer vision algorithms output a single probability or classification label without explaining the underlying reasoning. Medical officers and specialists rightfully hesitate to act on uninterpretable predictions when deciding whether to refer or treat a patient.
5. **Infrastructure and Connectivity Gaps**: Reliable, high-speed internet connectivity is often absent in remote villages and community outreach camps, causing conventional cloud-dependent telemedicine platforms to fail.

---

## 3. Proposed Solution

NetraSaarthi provides an integrated, human-centered screening ecosystem that connects rural frontline health workers with secondary and tertiary ophthalmic care:

- **Point-of-Care Frontline Screening**: A touch-friendly, accessible interface enabling ASHA/ANM workers to register patients, capture or upload fundus photographs, and receive immediate automated screening feedback.
- **Explainable AI Attention Maps**: An explainability engine that highlights the precise retinal regions (such as microaneurysms, hemorrhages, or exudates) that influenced the model's classification, demystifying the prediction for both health workers and clinicians.
- **Explainable Pre-Inference Quality Gate**: An automated heuristic evaluation step that assesses incoming photographs for focus, illumination, and contrast before AI processing, halting inference and prompting recaptures when images are degraded.
- **Zero-Data-Loss Offline Architecture**: A Progressive Web Application (PWA) that caches the entire application shell and queues patient screenings locally when internet connectivity is unavailable, automatically synchronizing with the central FastAPI backend when online.
- **Structured Tele-Ophthalmology Referral**: A seamless handoff workflow that enables district eye specialists to review queued cases, inspect Grad-CAM visualizations, override or confirm findings, and generate official, printable Ayushman Bharat–compatible referral slips.

## 6. Key Features

1. **Pre-Inference Image Quality Gate**:
   - Analyzes four explainable physical heuristics prior to inference: resolution, mean luminance, contrast, and Laplacian blur variance.
   - Prevents fabricated or degraded predictions by categorizing images as `GOOD` or `RETAKE_REQUIRED`.
2. **5-Class ICDR Severity Grading**:
   - Classifies fundus imagery into the standard International Clinical Diabetic Retinopathy (ICDR) scale: Grade 0 (No DR), Grade 1 (Mild), Grade 2 (Moderate), Grade 3 (Severe), and Grade 4 (Proliferative DR).
3. **Interactive Multi-Layer Retinal Visualizer**:
   - Provides live opacity controls across multiple diagnostic layers: Original Fundus, Grad-CAM Attention Heatmap, Red-Free (Green Filter) view, and Lesion Markers.
4. **Automated Lesion Quantification Assistance**:
   - Highlights indicative lesion patterns (microaneurysms, hemorrhages, lipid exudates, cotton-wool spots, and neovascularization) to assist clinical scrutiny.
5. **Bilingual Voice Guidance**:
   - Web Speech API integration that speaks diagnostic results and lifestyle counseling aloud in Hindi and English, facilitating communication with non-literate patients.
6. **Offline-First PWA with Secure Local Vault**:
   - Complete application shell caching via Service Worker (`sw.js`).
   - Local queuing in browser storage (`localStorage`) without storing sensitive personal identification (PII).
   - Automatic background synchronization upon reconnection, complemented by manual sync triggers.
7. **Official ABDM-Ready Referral Slip**:
   - One-click generation of printable referral slips complete with patient identifiers, eye scanned, ICDR stage, ICD-10 codes, QR verification codes, and doctor sign-off fields.
8. **Longitudinal DR Progression Tracking**:
   - Interactive charts illustrating disease progression and visual acuity trends across consecutive screening visits.

## 8. Explainable AI (XAI) Approach

In medical applications, understandability is critical. Conventional deep learning models operate as "black boxes," offering high predictive confidence without revealing the features that informed the decision.

```
                    ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
                    │               Explainability Pipeline                  │
                    └──────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────┘
                                               │
               ┌───────────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────┐
               ▼                                                               ▼
  ┌─────────────────────────┐                                     ┌─────────────────────────┐
  │ Gradient-Weighted Class │                                     │ Interactive Multi-Layer │
  │   Activation Mapping    │                                     │     Inspection UI       │
  │        (Grad-CAM)       │                                     │                         │
  │ • Extracts gradients at │                                     │ • Original Fundus       │
  │   final conv layer      │                                     │ • Heatmap Opacity Slider│
  │ • Highlights micro-     │                                     │ • Red-Free Green Channel│
  │   vascular pathologies  │                                     │ • Lesion Marker Overlay │
  └─────────────────────────┘                                     └─────────────────────────┘
```

### 8.1 Technical Implementation
- **Target Layer**: NetraSaarthi hooks into the final convolutional feature extractor (`features.8` of the EfficientNet-B0 backbone).
- **Gradient Backpropagation**: During inference, gradients of the predicted class score are pooled across spatial dimensions to calculate importance weights for each convolutional feature map.
- **Heatmap Synthesis**: The weighted feature maps are combined, rectified with ReLU (isolating features that positively contribute to the class), normalized, and color-mapped to project an attention heatmap back onto the retinal plane.

### 8.2 Clinical Utility
- The attention heatmap visually directs the clinician's gaze to microaneurysms, hemorrhages, or new vessel formations.
- Clinicians can instantly verify whether the model is focusing on genuine clinical pathology or spurious camera artifacts.
- A prominent disclaimer makes clear that the attention visualization is an assistive aid and not a definitive lesion segmentation map.

## 9. Offline-First Approach

Because primary healthcare sub-centers in rural India frequently operate beyond the reach of reliable cellular towers, NetraSaarthi implements a robust offline-first architecture:

1. **Application Shell Pre-Caching**:
   - A dedicated Service Worker (`sw.js`) pre-caches core static assets (`/`, `/index.html`, `/manifest.webmanifest`, CSS, JS bundles, and vector icons).
   - The user interface loads and renders instantly even with zero network connectivity.
2. **Local Vault Data Minimization**:
   - Screenings conducted in offline mode are saved directly to the device's browser `localStorage`.
   - **Privacy Boundary**: To protect patient confidentiality, personal identifying information (PII) such as full names, residential addresses, and phone numbers are strictly omitted from the local queue. Records are referenced solely through non-PII clinical tokens (e.g., `PAT-2026-001`).
3. **Resilient Synchronization Cycle**:
   - A global online/offline event listener continuously monitors connectivity.
   - When the connection is restored, NetraSaarthi automatically checks the health of the FastAPI backend (`GET /health`), synchronizes pending records sequentially via `POST /screenings`, clears successfully uploaded queue items, and updates the local status indicators.

## 10. Human-in-the-Loop Approach

NetraSaarthi is deliberately architected around the principle that **artificial intelligence must support, never supplant, healthcare professionals**:

```
 ┌───────────────────────────┐         ┌───────────────────────────┐         ┌───────────────────────────┐
 │   ASHA / ANM Worker       │         │   NetraSaarthi AI         │         │   Specialist Doctor       │
 │                           │         │                           │         │                           │
 │ • Captures fundus image   │ ──────▶ │ • Heuristic quality check │ ──────▶ │ • Inspects raw fundus     │
 │ • Explains process to     │         │ • Predicts ICDR stage     │         │ • Evaluates Grad-CAM map  │
 │   patient                 │         │ • Generates Grad-CAM map  │         │ • Overrides or confirms   │
 │ • Delivers audio advice   │         │ • Calculates confidence   │         │ • Signs official referral │
 └───────────────────────────┘         └───────────────────────────┘         └───────────────────────────┘
```

- **Frontline Triage Support**: ASHA/ANM workers use the AI output solely to determine referral urgency and provide basic preventive counseling, not to declare a medical diagnosis.
- **Specialist Clinical Override**: Within the Doctor Dashboard, the consulting ophthalmologist can:
  - Review the original fundus photograph independently of the model prediction.
  - Inspect the Grad-CAM attention heatmap to understand the model's hypothesis.
  - Manually change or override the disease grade based on their professional judgment.
  - Input custom clinical observations, treatment recommendations, and follow-up intervals.
- **Formal Sign-Off**: The final diagnosis recorded in the patient's record is explicitly attributed to the reviewing clinician, ensuring clear medical accountability.

## 11. Referral-Oriented Workflow

Rather than acting as an isolated diagnostic test, NetraSaarthi is structured to facilitate timely and appropriate patient escalation across the tiers of the public health system:

| ICDR Severity Stage | Clinical Risk Level | Recommended Referral Action | Public Health Pathway |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Grade 0: No DR** | Non-Referable | Routine annual rescreening. | Ayushman Arogya Mandir (Village PHC / Sub-Center) |
| **Grade 1: Mild NPDR** | Non-Referable | Glycemic and blood pressure counseling; 6–12 month follow-up. | Primary Health Center Medical Officer |
| **Grade 2: Moderate NPDR** | Referable | Routine specialist evaluation within 4–8 weeks. | Community Health Center (CHC) / District Hospital |
| **Grade 3: Severe NPDR** | Referable (Urgent) | Urgent comprehensive evaluation within 2–4 weeks; diagnostic angiography consideration. | District Hospital Eye Department |
| **Grade 4: Proliferative DR** | Referable (Emergency) | Immediate emergency vitreoretinal referral within 24–48 hours for laser PRP or anti-VEGF therapy. | Tertiary Eye Hospital / Medical College |

### Official Printable Referral Slip
When an eye specialist or frontline worker confirms a referable condition, NetraSaarthi generates a standardized, printable referral document containing:
- Patient clinical ID and demographic summary.
- Eye examined (Right Eye OD / Left Eye OS).
- AI screening grade vs. Doctor-confirmed diagnostic grade.
- Standardized ICD-10 diagnostic coding (e.g., `E11.319` for Non-Proliferative DR).
- Automated referral urgency classification and recommended follow-up timeline.
- Cryptographic / QR code for referral slip authenticity verification.
- Signature block for the examining or reviewing medical officer.

## 14. Expected Impact

Implementing NetraSaarthi across rural healthcare networks can generate substantial public health, economic, and operational benefits:

- **Prevention of Avoidable Blindness**: Facilitates early detection of diabetic retinopathy during non-proliferative stages, when glycemic control and timely interventions can prevent severe, permanent vision loss.
- **Optimized Specialist Workload**: Prevents tertiary hospital ophthalmologists from being overwhelmed by normal examinations, reserving specialist time for patients requiring urgent laser photocoagulation or surgical intervention.
- **Grassroots Healthcare Empowerment**: Equips community ASHA and ANM workers with accessible technology, enhancing their clinical capabilities and fostering greater health awareness in rural villages.
- **Reduction in Out-of-Pocket Expenses**: Saves rural families substantial financial burdens associated with repeated, unnecessary travel, lost daily wages, and private clinic fees.
- **Strengthening Public Health Data Infrastructure**: Generates standardized, structured digital screening data that aligns with national non-communicable disease (NCD) tracking goals.

## 15. Limitations

To maintain scientific rigor, clinical honesty, and ethical compliance, the current limitations of the NetraSaarthi prototype must be clearly recognized:

1. **Non-Clinical Validation Status**: NetraSaarthi is an academic and hackathon proof-of-concept. It has **not** undergone prospective multi-center clinical trials, nor does it possess medical device regulatory clearance from the Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation (CDSCO) or equivalent bodies.
2. **Dependence on Input Image Quality**: The system relies on reasonable fundus illumination and clarity. Severe cataracts, dense corneal opacities, uncooperative patients, or uncorrected camera defocus will trigger retake requests and may require manual specialist evaluation.
3. **Single-Condition Focus**: The model is trained specifically for Diabetic Retinopathy. It does not autonomously detect, classify, or rule out other serious ophthalmic pathologies that may be present on a fundus scan, such as glaucoma, age-related macular degeneration (AMD), or retinal vein occlusions.
4. **Hardware and Capture Standardization**: The prototype has been evaluated against standardized public datasets; performance across highly diverse, ultra-low-cost smartphone attachment lenses may vary and requires field-specific calibration.

## 16. Future Scope

The development roadmap for NetraSaarthi envisions expanding from a prototype decision-support tool into a comprehensive rural tele-ophthalmology infrastructure:

1. **On-Device Edge AI Deployment**:
   - Quantize the PyTorch model into ONNX Runtime or TensorFlow Lite formats to execute inference directly on low-cost Android tablets or smartphones without requiring any server infrastructure.
2. **Expanded Multi-Disease Ophthalmic Screening**:
   - Incorporate secondary deep learning heads to screen simultaneously for Glaucoma (via automated optic cup-to-disc ratio estimation), Age-Related Macular Degeneration (drusen detection), and Hypertensive Retinopathy.
3. **Deep National Health Portal Integration**:
   - Connect directly with India's **Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM)** APIs for seamless health record generation (ABHA linkage) and automated synchronization with the National NCD Portal.
4. **Expanded Regional Language Voice Counselors**:
   - Broaden the bilingual voice assistant to include major regional Indian languages (e.g., Bengali, Telugu, Tamil, Marathi, Kannada, and Odia) to support diverse rural communities nationwide.
5. **Prospective Clinical Pilot Studies**:
   - Partner with regional eye institutes (such as Aravind Eye Care System, LV Prasad Eye Institute, or Dr. R.P. Centre at AIIMS) to conduct rigorous prospective clinical validation studies assessing sensitivity, specificity, and real-world health worker usability.
